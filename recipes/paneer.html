Paneer

Description

Paneer is a fresh Indian cottage cheese known for its soft texture, mild flavor, and versatility. It is widely used in Indian dishes such as Paneer Butter Masala, Shahi Paneer, Palak Paneer, and Paneer Tikka. Rich in protein and calcium, paneer is a popular ingredient in vegetarian cuisine.

Ingredients

  • Paneer
  • Onions
  • Tomatoes
  • Butter

Steps

  1. Cut the paneer into small cubes.
  2. Chop the onions and tomatoes.
  3. Heat butter in a pan and sauté the onions and tomatoes.
  4. Add spices and cook until the mixture thickens.
  5. Add the paneer cubes and mix gently.
  6. Cook for 5-7 minutes and serve hot.
