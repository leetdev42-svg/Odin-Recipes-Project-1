Tandoori Chicken

Description

Tandoori Chicken is a classic Indian dish made with chicken marinated in yogurt and aromatic spices, then traditionally cooked in a clay oven called a tandoor. It is famous for its smoky flavor, vibrant color, and tender texture.

Ingredients

  • Chicken
  • Yogurt
  • Red Chili Powder
  • Garam Masala

Steps

  1. Clean the chicken and make small cuts in the pieces.
  2. Prepare a marinade using yogurt and spices.
  3. Coat the chicken thoroughly with the marinade.
  4. Refrigerate for at least 2 hours.
  5. Grill or bake the chicken until fully cooked.
  6. Serve hot with lemon wedges and mint chutney.
