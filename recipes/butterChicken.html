Butter Chicken

Description

Butter Chicken is a popular Indian dish made with tender chicken pieces cooked in a creamy tomato-based gravy with butter and aromatic spices. It is known for its rich flavor and smooth texture.

Ingredients

  • Chicken
  • Tomatoes
  • Butter
  • Fresh Cream

Steps

  1. Marinate the chicken with yogurt and spices.
  2. Cook the chicken until tender.
  3. Heat butter in a pan and prepare a tomato-based gravy.
  4. Add spices and fresh cream to the gravy.
  5. Add the cooked chicken and mix well.
  6. Simmer for a few minutes and serve hot.
